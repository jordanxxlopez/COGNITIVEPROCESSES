# PSYC301 experiment videos

Additive video collection. Existing source slides, all transcript versions, quiz items, note/read keys and pre-existing media are preserved. Frames are conventional Python/Pillow drawings encoded by FFmpeg; no generative images, Blender or local Mac execution. Brief displays use 60 fps. Browser refresh, frame drops, scaling and color calibration prevent treating these movies as measurement instruments.

## Scope and fidelity

The current and archived experiment lists were reviewed. Included films cover verified visual sequences, available original stimulus figures, course visual demonstrations, and clearly identified worked puzzle solutions. The two existing research supplementary movies are preserved as related task variants with original attribution, not relabeled as the historic experiments. No participant reaction times, eye paths, mental images, error rates or responses are invented.

Other entries include auditory-only procedures, historical conditioning/lesion cases, observation studies, interview/questionnaire scenarios, and visual tasks whose exact materials are not verified here. They retain their full existing content; a result graph or apparatus picture is not recast as the participant's display. This collection is not a claim to have recovered every original experimental stimulus set.
