# Module I teaching contract

Use the existing LING406 interface and the four agreed teaching options. The
course sources determine the content. PSYC301 and LING285 supply writing
examples, never facts to import into LING406.

## Shared writing requirements

- Teach the actual source page in connected, complete sentences. Introduce the
  concept directly, explain the mechanism or relationship, and use its actual
  example to make the distinction understandable.
- Main bullets contain teaching, not fragment headings. Use natural supporting
  sub-bullets and restrained bold for the important terms and findings. Let the
  source determine the number of bullets and the length. Avoid a repeated quota
  or obligatory setup/result/interpretation sequence.
- Retain technical terminology and explain it in plain language. Preserve
  researchers, years, conditions and example details when the source provides
  them and they matter to understanding.
- Explain what participants did, what differed between conditions, what was
  observed, and why that observation supports a claim. Tell this naturally,
  rather than using report labels or a checklist.
- Explain meaningful original visual information. For a graph, diagram, table,
  transcription or error example, identify what its labels represent and explain
  the relationship or change. Retain quantities that define conditions or key
  findings. Avoid reciting incidental numbers and decorative details.
- No presentation narration, such as "the slide shows," "the lecture says,"
  "here we see," or "as discussed earlier." No roadmaps or references to later
  slides. No stock concluding limitations, generic caveats or filler.
- Do not append an invented limitation to every study. A qualification actually
  taught by the source belongs within its specific explanation. Preserve the
  meaning of evidence without overstating it.
- Do not use "need not" or evaluative "lawful." LING406 teaching prose uses no
  colons or semicolons. Original source text remains verbatim.
- Preserve every source page, including title, divider and logistical pages.
  A genuinely noninstructional page may have an empty transcript. Do not invent
  explanations to fill one or silently omit it.
- "No speech error" in the user's writing-preference request does not authorize
  deleting the Speech Errors I and II course material. Explain each actual
  speech-error stimulus precisely, keeping intended and produced forms distinct.

## Four editions

1. **PSYC301 v20-style teaching** gives complete parent explanations, with
   supporting sentences that explain why the concept works and what its example
   means. This is natural spoken teaching, not a terse summary.
2. **Original C · Version 2** gives clear direct explanations in the source's
   conceptual order. It can be shorter than v20 while retaining the mechanism,
   source terminology and necessary example distinctions.
3. **D · Version 2** offers another individually authored teaching presentation.
   The LING285 reference retains the underlying C content while integrating
   source-grounded teaching additions. Do not create a weaker edition by merely
   deleting paragraphs, or claim a recording exists when none was supplied.
4. **Original C · Version 3** explains important visual meanings more explicitly
   where the source page calls for that. Add relevant labels, stimulus mappings,
   comparisons and their implications, not repeated decorative walkthroughs.

Shared facts may recur across editions. Their purposes are different framing and
explanatory depth, not different scientific claims. Do not force wording changes
where they reduce precision or pad a simple page.

## Verification

Review the original visual and extracted content before authoring. Check the
finished explanation against both, especially IPA, morpheme boundaries, brain
labels, speech-error stimuli, experimental conditions and graphs. Retain source
page references and record unreadable or unavailable media honestly. Static
renderings do not establish the content of hidden animation or linked audio.
