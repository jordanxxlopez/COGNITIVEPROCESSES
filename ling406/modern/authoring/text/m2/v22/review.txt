54|The **Revised Hierarchical Model** of **Kroll and Stewart (1990, 1994)** explains interactions between L1 and L2 in terms of **spreading activation**, which means that activating one representation partly activates the representations linked to it. >> L1 words are strongly linked to concepts and L2 words are linked more weakly. || When the task of **Marian and Spivey (2003)** is done in L2, the concept for “speaker” **partially activates the Russian word for “matches,”** spichki, because the two words begin with the same sounds. >> This activity **feeds back to the conceptual activation** for “matches,” which leads to **eye movements toward the L1 competitor**. || When the task is done in L1, the **conceptual links with L2** might be **too weak** to create this kind of interference reliably, so interference runs from the first language into the second and not reliably the other way.
