# PSYC301 stimulus motion audit

The 36-item gallery was reviewed by task type and against its manifest and generated frame validations. Tasks requiring a brief exposure, cue, mask, serial order, changing pattern, movement, or worked sequence use timed video frames. Published shape pairs remain still for Shepard and Metzler's mental-rotation task; the Müller-Lyer illusion compares still lines; the color-matching example is a static display. These are not animated because movement is not part of those stimuli.

The Metcalfe and Wiebe chain entry was the clear gap. Its old video repeated the source figure for the entire clip, even though the accompanying method described a solution. It now shows the original course puzzle first, animates opening all three links of one chain, moves those links to join the other three chains into a closed loop, and displays the 6¢ opening plus 9¢ closing cost. The solution is labeled as an illustrative reconstruction, not a participant recording or warmth-rating trace.

The triangle solution remains animated. Other solution demonstrations already model relevant movement or transitions, including the candle, two strings, Tower of Hanoi, and water-jug examples. Verification decoded all 36 clips and passed their dimensions, frame counts, duration and existing stimulus-specific checks.
