# PSYC301 Version 22 — final board review

The user’s examples describe the rule for the entire edition. This revision therefore compares every Version 20 and Version 21 teaching passage in individual collections 09–14 and checks the corresponding original evidence. Version 22 uses Version 21 as the base, adding natural source-backed researcher names, years and study attribution throughout. It retains the existing page order, title/divider roles, bullet structure and following-slide presentation.

## What changed

Research attributions now appear in the teaching prose. Single authors use Name (year), two authors use Name and Name (year), and larger groups use Name and colleagues (year). Experiments are described as experiments, while model proposals retain their appropriate verbs. Baddeley (2000) is restored in both the model overview and the episodic-buffer explanation. Other restored references include the memory experiments, problem-solving research, sacred-value studies, decision experiments and mental-model work recorded in the page-level ledgers. A source-only reference to Baron and Spranca (1997), absent from Version 20, is included too.

Version 21 explicitly rejected years. The new edition permits parenthetical citation years, including multiple years, while retaining the remaining direct, complete-sentence teaching rules. It does not reintroduce measured figure inventories or turn the notes into label stubs. Titles, dividers and the empty lab placeholder remain present without invented teaching.

Three inherited ambiguities were corrected only in the new edition: farm/library totals do not establish farmer/librarian population counts; accepting a positive ultimatum offer is the monetary-maximization prediction rather than a universal definition of rational choice; and anterior-insula activity is associated with rejection rather than establishing a causal mechanism. The ledgers distinguish these changes from attribution insertions.

## Limits and resolutions

Some supplied materials name no researcher or provide no date. Examples include several classic puzzles, some demonstrations, Tulving’s quotation, and some change-detection figures. These gaps are documented in the evidence files. No unsupported name or year was invented. The source’s Chi date and other source-specific terminology are retained with explicit provenance, without representing an outside historical verification. Paired Dehghani references are retained without inventing a result split between papers.
